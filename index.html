How BankStore matches businesses with banks, EMIs and payment providers

BankStore is the AI bridge between banks and businesses globally. It helps a company find a bank or payment provider that fits its profile, then open the account. This page explains the method in plain terms.

The problem

Banks and payment providers each decide which countries, industries and business models they will onboard. These rules are rarely public. A company that applies blindly can spend weeks on applications that were never going to be approved.

The data

BankStore works with a database of 1,400+ providers, including 290+ banks and 910+ acquirers. They cover 60+ countries and 95+ currencies.

The method

  1. One company profile. The business describes itself once: where it is registered, what it does, which currencies it uses and where its payments go.
  2. Matching. The AI engine compares that profile with what each provider is known to accept. See bank matching.
  3. Corridors. For companies that move money between countries, the payment routes matter as much as the bank. See cross-border payments.
  4. Account opening. The company chooses a provider and gets help with opening the account.

FAQ

Is BankStore a bank?

No. BankStore is an AI tool that connects businesses with banks and payment providers.

Is it a marketplace?

No. It matches a company profile with providers that are likely to accept it, rather than listing offers for comparison.

Who is it for?

Startups, SMEs and larger companies that need a business account, merchant acquiring or cross-border payments.
